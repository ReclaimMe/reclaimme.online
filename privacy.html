Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Our Commitment

ReclaimMe is built on privacy. All analysis happens in your browser. We don't track you, sell your data, or store your content on our servers.

What We Don't Collect

  • Your social media content
  • Your photos or posts
  • Your analysis results
  • Your personal information

What We Do Collect

  • Email address (only if you purchase a license)
  • Basic app analytics (crashes, feature usage)
  • License activation data

Browser-Based Processing

All AI analysis happens directly in your browser using WebLLM models. We have no access to your data.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Drive: Used only when you choose to export reports (optional)
  • LemonSqueezy: Processes payments and manages licenses
  • Chrome/Firefox: Extension stores host our browser extension
