Data Deletion Instructions

How to delete your data from ReclaimMe application

🔒 Privacy First: Your Data Stays Local

Important: ReclaimMe stores ALL your data locally on your computer. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers. Your social media content, analysis results, and personal information never leave your device.

🗑️ Complete Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over deletion. Follow these steps to permanently remove all ReclaimMe data:

Option 1: Uninstall the Application

  1. Uninstall ReclaimMe from your computer using your operating system's standard uninstall process
  2. Delete application data folder (see paths below)
  3. Verification: All your data is now permanently deleted

Option 2: Manual Data Deletion

  1. Close the ReclaimMe application completely
  2. Navigate to the data directory for your operating system
  3. Delete the entire ReclaimMe folder
  4. Empty your trash/recycle bin to permanently delete

Data Storage Locations

ReclaimMe stores data in the following locations:

Windows:
%APPDATA%\ReclaimMe\
C:\Users\[username]\AppData\Roaming\ReclaimMe\

macOS:
~/Library/Application Support/ReclaimMe/

Linux:
~/.config/ReclaimMe/

What Data is Deleted

Deleting the ReclaimMe folder removes:

  • All downloaded social media posts and photos
  • AI analysis results and reputation scores
  • Application settings and preferences
  • Local database with your content metadata
  • Archived content in all categories
  • Export files and reports
  • Application logs and usage data

🔗 Social Media Connections

ReclaimMe connects to social media platforms using your credentials, but does not store them on our servers:

Revoking Access

  1. Facebook: Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → Remove ReclaimMe
  2. Instagram: No OAuth connection - only username/password used locally
  3. Twitter/X: Go to Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Revoke ReclaimMe access
Note: Since ReclaimMe uses local authentication, revoking access from social media platforms primarily prevents future connections. Your locally stored data must still be deleted manually as described above.

📋 Data Processing Information

What We Process

  • Social Media Content: Posts, photos, comments, and metadata
  • AI Analysis: Risk assessments and reputation scoring
  • Usage Metrics: Application performance and feature usage
  • Error Logs: Technical information for debugging

Processing Location

All data processing occurs:

  • Locally on your device using AI models
  • Without internet connection for analysis
  • Without sending data to external servers
  • Under your complete control and ownership

⏱️ Retention Policy

Since all data is stored locally:

  • No automatic deletion: Data persists until you manually delete it
  • No server storage: We cannot delete data remotely
  • Your control: You decide when and what to delete
  • Export available: You can export your data before deletion
